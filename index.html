Meet Vertifold

Good puzzles start
with a simple idea.

Vertifold Studios is an independent mobile game studio founded by Jordan Turley. We make games inspired by graph theory: the mathematics of points, connections, and the surprising things they can do together.

No math degree required. Just a little curiosity and the satisfaction of that moment when everything clicks.
