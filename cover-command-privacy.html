Who we are

This privacy policy explains how Cover Command handles information. The game is developed by Jordan Turley, operating as Vertifold Studios. Existing store listings or app identifiers may still show Jordan Turley or the earlier personal branding. For privacy questions, contact ztizzlegaming@gmail.com.

Cover Command is in development. This policy describes the current development builds and applies when you use a build provided by us. It will be updated if the released game handles data differently.

Information and its uses

Game data on your device. The app stores progress, completed levels, stars, coins, power-up inventory, daily reward dates, and preferences locally to operate the game and remember your choices. There is no player account, and we do not run a server that receives your saved game. Your device’s backup service may copy this information according to your settings.

Advertising data. The app integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). When ads are requested or shown, Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from them), device or advertising identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. These are used to deliver and measure ads, provide advertising-related analytics, prevent abuse, and maintain the ad service. Data handled depends on platform, permissions, consent, SDK version, and ad configuration.

Support correspondence. If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use these to answer you, investigate problems, and handle privacy requests. Do not include sensitive information in a bug report.

The app does not ask you to register, enter payment information, or grant access to your contacts, microphone, camera, or precise device location. We do not add a separate analytics or account service to the game; advertising SDKs perform their own measurement.

Third parties and sharing

Google and advertising partners process ad-related information under their own policies and applicable agreements. We do not receive a copy of your full advertising profile. We use an email provider to receive and respond to support messages. Apple or Google may handle app distribution and device backups through their own services.

We do not sell saved game files or support messages, or provide them to data brokers. Ad-related sharing may be considered a “sale,” “sharing,” or targeted advertising under some privacy laws. Your available controls are described below. We may disclose information we hold if legally required or necessary to protect users, prevent abuse, or defend legal rights.

Third-party services must protect data as required by applicable law and our agreements; their independent practices are explained in their policies. Relevant resources:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps and sites using its services
  • Google AdMob advertising technology providers
  • Apple Privacy Policy

Privacy choices and deletion

iPhone and iPad: You can review or withdraw tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The app may request permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. Declining tracking does not prevent access to the game. Deleting the app removes its local storage; choosing “Offload App” may keep that storage.

Android: Use your device’s advertising privacy controls to reset or delete an advertising ID or manage ad personalization, where available. Use Settings → Apps → Cover Command → Storage → Clear storage to remove local app data; labels vary by device. Uninstalling also removes local app storage, but backup copies may remain.

Backups: To remove cloud backup copies, manage the relevant backup through Apple or Google. Reinstalling the app may restore backed-up data. We cannot delete backups held under your personal platform account.

Advertising: Device tracking or personalization choices do not necessarily remove ads or stop all ad-related data processing. Use the controls available on the ad itself or the provider’s privacy tools to manage provider-held information. Device tracking permission is separate from any regional consent choices.

Contact us: Email ztizzlegaming@gmail.com with “Cover Command privacy request” to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or an objection to processing of information we hold. No account is required. Explain what you want us to do; we may request limited information to verify the request. We can delete support correspondence we hold, subject to legal obligations, but cannot locate or remotely remove device-only saves or provider-held advertising records.

Depending on your location, you may also have rights to portability, withdrawal of consent, or opting out of targeted advertising, sale, or sharing. We will handle requests as required by applicable law. You may contact your local data protection authority if you have a concern.

Retention and security

Local game data remains until it is reset, cleared, or removed, subject to device backup behavior. We keep support messages while handling your request and for up to 12 months after it is resolved, unless you ask for earlier deletion or a legal obligation or ongoing dispute requires longer retention. Advertising providers set their own retention periods under their published policies; we cannot specify or control all of those periods.

We limit access to support correspondence and use the security features provided by our service providers. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Service providers may process information outside your country, subject to their applicable safeguards.

Children’s privacy

Cover Command is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children under 13 to provide personal information directly to us. Advertising services may process device-related data as described above; parents and guardians should review device privacy controls and supervise use as appropriate. Store age ratings describe content and do not mean that an app has no advertising data processing.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact ztizzlegaming@gmail.com so we can investigate and delete information we hold where required. Please do not include additional sensitive information about a child in your initial message.

Policy updates and contact

We may update this policy when the game, service providers, or data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Material changes requiring notice or consent will be handled as required by applicable law.
