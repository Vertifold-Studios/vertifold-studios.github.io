Website privacy notice

Effective and last updated: October 24, 2026

This website is operated by Jordan Turley as Vertifold Studios and hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use analytics scripts, advertising trackers, embedded social media, web fonts from third parties, cookies, or browser storage. All illustrations and site assets are served with the site.

GitHub receives technical information, including visitors’ IP addresses, when serving the website for security and operational purposes. Its processing and retention are described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not run a separate visitor database.

External links take you to services such as app stores, Google, Apple, or Wikipedia, which have their own privacy practices. Clicking an email link opens your email application; nothing is sent until you choose to send it. When you contact us, we receive your address, message, and attachments. We use these to respond and retain correspondence for up to 12 months after resolution, unless earlier deletion is requested or longer retention is required by law or an ongoing dispute.

Privacy requests

Email ztizzlegaming@gmail.com for access, correction, or deletion of correspondence we hold, or questions about this site. State whether your request concerns the website or a particular game. We may need limited information to verify your request. Rights, including the ability to complain to a data protection authority, depend on applicable law. We cannot erase logs held independently by the hosting provider; please consult its privacy policy for its request process.
