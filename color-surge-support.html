Common questions

Is Color Surge available to download?

Yes. Color Surge is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad and on Google Play for Android.

How do I play?

Color each power station so that connected stations have different colors. Use the in-game tutorial to learn the controls and power-ups.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress, coins, stars, and power-ups are saved on your device. There is no Vertifold account or cross-platform cloud sync. Device backups may include app data depending on your system settings. We cannot restore local progress from a studio server.

How do I reset my progress?

Open the settings menu on the main screen and choose Reset Progress. This resets level progress, stars, coins, power-ups, and the daily reward streak. This action cannot be undone. To remove app storage, use Android’s Clear storage control or delete the app on iOS; offloading the app can keep its data.

An ad did not load, or my reward did not arrive. What can I do?

Check your connection and try again later. Ad availability varies, and a reward usually requires completing the rewarded ad. If the problem continues, email the game name, app version, device, approximate time, and what happened. Please do not send your advertising identifier.

How do I control advertising or tracking?

On iOS, review Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, use the advertising privacy controls in your device settings. These choices do not necessarily remove ads or stop all ad-related processing. See the game’s privacy policy for details.

The app crashes or a puzzle looks wrong. How do I report it?

Try closing and reopening the app, checking for updates, and restarting your device. Avoid deleting the app as a first step because you may lose progress. Send us steps to reproduce the issue, your device and OS version, the app version, and the level number if relevant.

How do I report an inappropriate ad?

Use the ad’s report or information control when available. You can also email us the advertiser, approximate time, device, and a screenshot with personal information removed. We can investigate reports but do not directly control every ad served by the advertising provider.

How do I ask about privacy or delete data?

Visit the privacy choices and deletion section. You can also email us with “Color Surge privacy request” in the subject. There are no player accounts to delete.

Color Surge is available now on iOS and Android. For privacy questions, read the Color Surge privacy policy.
